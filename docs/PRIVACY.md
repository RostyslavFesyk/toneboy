# toneboy Privacy Policy

toneboy does not collect, transmit, or sell personal data. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking.

With your permission, toneboy processes your Mac’s system audio to apply the equalizer and draw its live spectrum. Audio is processed in memory on your Mac and is not recorded to disk or sent anywhere. toneboy may play a short, quiet test signal to verify that capture works. It does not enable the microphone stream.

Your equalizer and appearance settings are stored locally on your Mac. You can stop processing with the equalizer switch or revoke system audio access in macOS System Settings under Privacy & Security.

If you choose to post on GitHub for support, your post is handled by GitHub and may be public. Please omit private information and audio. For questions about this policy, visit [toneboy Support](SUPPORT.md).
